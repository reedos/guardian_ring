Current scope

The story is a static look prototype with Blender-rendered, representative scenes and selected public reference figures. It is ready for visual review. The interactive explorer still contains placeholder scenes.

The scenario controls store choices and test the viewer contract. They do not calculate orbital, optical, thermal or detection performance. The physics engine comes after the source and look reviews; published civil instrument values remain attached to the named instrument.

Illustration geometry

Representative / not to scale

The prototype’s geometry, layout, satellite counts and positions, and ray paths are chosen to explain component roles. They do not describe a real constellation, sensor performance, or physical dimensions.

The hardware stills use a shared black background, amber light-path highlights and a blue cold-stage color. These colors and the exploded spacing help separate component roles. They do not represent measured temperature, an optical prescription or a real system’s construction.

The explorer’s placeholder geometry has no physical scale. Its scenario selections remain interface placeholders.

How this is checked

Type checks, unit tests and a strict claim audit run alongside browser gates for the parts, framing, controls, camera paths, rendering quality, performance and links. The static look is also checked at desktop and phone sizes.
