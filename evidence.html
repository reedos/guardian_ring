The labels

Spec
The maker or a standards body publishes this figure for the named product or standard.
Vendor
The vendor’s own comparison or performance figure, attributed to it. The site has not checked it independently.
Reported
Stated by a named party outside a formal specification: a government agency, a researcher, an analyst, the trade press, or an operator in an informal statement such as a post.
Calc.
Calculated by this site’s model from the scenario and the figures it cites. The method page shows how.
Assumed
A value the model chooses where no single published figure applies. The method page says why.
