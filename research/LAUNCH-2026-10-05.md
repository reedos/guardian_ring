# Public launch — 10/05/2026

Reed authorized launching The Guardian Ring at https://reedos.dev/guardian_ring/,
adding its GitHub website link, and integrating it into the Reedos.dev portfolio.

Public pages now allow indexing and following. The sitemap lists all six public
pages; the portfolio sitemap links them as well. This launch does not claim that
search engines have already indexed the site or that physical-device testing is complete.
