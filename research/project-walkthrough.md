# Project walkthrough and corrections

Reviewed 10/03/2026. **Status: implementation complete for the items below; integrated browser acceptance pending.** This note describes code and authored assets, not a passing gate report.

The review follows Intelligence Factory's component inspection, evidence, navigation and Blender-asset conventions. All spacecraft packaging, routes, display activity and presentation timing remain representative. Public ABI and TIRS-2 descriptions establish named civil component roles; they do not establish military packaging or performance. Source verification and orbital calculations are recorded in [mission-motion-review.md](mission-motion-review.md) and the existing fact ledgers.

Hidden orbit families and closed electronics interiors suppress inapplicable pins. |

The camera continues to use authored inspection views and clearance planning. Manual inspection, camera manipulation and navigation can take control from the guided experience. Hidden tabs and source overlays suspend activity. Reduced-motion readers retain stationary/manual progression rather than compulsory orbital camera movement. These behaviors require final integrated UI verification.

## Engineering and evidence checks

- Orbital periods and the nonuniform HEO motion use the existing physics model. Physical speed claims use the independent orbital calculation and evidence registry, never the compressed drawing coordinates.
- Payload v14 preserves the v13 inspection geometry: both contain 86,348 triangles; the CPU comparison found identical bounds and no canonical triangle differences at its stated drawing precision. Only explicit pivot transforms change during the scan demonstration.
- Mechanism tests exercise fixed mounting transforms, moving optical groups, exact restoration of the inspection pose and the reflection law. Teaching-program tests resolve the new civil claim keys and their component-focus mappings.
- No orbital thrust trail, operational sensor coverage, classified performance, military tasking schedule or processing latency was introduced.
