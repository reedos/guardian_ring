## What is NOT public (must be Calc./Assumed or omitted)

- **Exact SBIRS/Next-Gen OPIR sensor sensitivity, detection thresholds, noise-equivalent irradiance, or minimum detectable plume intensity.** No public source gives the actual performance specs that determine what size/type of missile plume is detectable at what range — this is classified.
